About

Sprites are palette-indexed character rows. Colour lives in the palette, not the sprite — so recolouring a whole world for a season, for night, or for a different biome is one palette, not a second copy of the art. Everything here is shared: any Forge app reading the same library sees your edits.

About

There are no audio files in Forge. A sound is a dozen numbers describing a small Web Audio graph, built by the same shared engine the game plays it with — so what you hear here is exactly what you hear in the game. Sounds live in the content store beside crops and recipes; any Forge app reading it hears your edits.

How this works

The model does not draw pixels — asked for a grid it returns an empty or ragged one. It is asked for a short list of shapes instead, and those are drawn here. That is why the results look like pixel art rather than noise, and why a floor plan, a sprite and a ship all use the same prompt shape. A world breaks that pattern on purpose: it is not pixels, just the six fields Janhollow's worlds type wants.

Nothing is saved until you press Save, so a bad result costs a button press and nothing else.
