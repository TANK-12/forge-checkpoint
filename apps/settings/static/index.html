Themes

A theme sets the colours, font, corners, wallpaper (some of them move) and home layout in one click. Alt+T switches from anywhere in Forge. Undo puts back what the last pick replaced.
They go in ~/.config/forge/theme/ and change nothing by themselves: source them from your Hyprland, kitty or foot config to follow the theme. Your own configs are never edited.
